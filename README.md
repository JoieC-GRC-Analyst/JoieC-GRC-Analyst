New Brunswick Responsible AI Framework (NB-RAF)

Creator & Steward | Draft 2026

NB-RAF is a practitioner-led, voluntary governance framework exploring how global AI governance principles can be translated into a practical context for New Brunswick and Atlantic Canada.

The draft draws from frameworks and principles including:

* NIST AI Risk Management Framework
* ISO/IEC 42001
* EU AI Act
* Canadian AI policy developments
* UNESCO AI ethics principles
* OCAP® principles
* UNDRIP
* AI security and adversarial-risk practices

NB-RAF is not legislation or an approved government standard. It is an open public consultation project intended to encourage practical discussion around responsible AI governance in New Brunswick and Atlantic Canada.
